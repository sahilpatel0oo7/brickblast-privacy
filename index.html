Privacy Policy for Brick Blast

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Brick Blast ("the app") is a free offline game published by Sahil ("the developer"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not request any device permissions or internet access.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your best score on your own device so it can be shown the next time you play. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
